# Step 2. Choose an app domain (5 minutes)

Pick something you know or care about: a club, a hobby, a job you've had, a place, a problem you've seen. Examples: a ramen-shop finder for your friends, a practice tracker for a band, a study-room finder, a climbing log.

You will invent realistic data for it in step 4, so you do not need a dataset. If you would rather use real public data, [DATASETS.md](../DATASETS.md) has direct links you can give the AI.

Write your domain at the top of [IDEA.md](../IDEA.md).
